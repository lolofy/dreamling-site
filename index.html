Dreamling

A virtual companion that is born from your sleep. For Apple Watch.

Getting started

Open Dreamling on your Apple Watch, allow Health access, and name your egg. Wear your watch to bed — each night you sleep warms the egg. After 7 nights, it hatches.

My egg isn't growing

Make sure Sleep Tracking is on (iPhone Health app → Sleep) and that you wear your watch overnight. Nights shorter than 2 hours don't count. Open Dreamling in the morning to sync.

Restoring Dreamling Plus

Open the Sleep page, tap Unlock, then "Restore purchases".

Changing Health access

Watch Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Dreamling, or the Health app on iPhone → Sharing → Apps.
