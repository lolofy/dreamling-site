Dreamling Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

In short: Dreamling reads your sleep and a few related Health readings to raise your virtual companion. All of it stays on your Apple Watch. We don't have servers, we don't collect your data, and we never sell, share or use it for advertising.

What Dreamling reads from Apple Health

Only with your permission, and only these types:

  • Sleep (time asleep, bedtime, wake time, sleep stages) — hatches and grows your pet, and powers the Sleep page.
  • Heart rate and heart rate variability (HRV) — shapes your pet's mood and notices when you've fallen asleep during "Tuck in".
  • Respiratory rate — shown on the Sleep page (Dreamling Plus).
  • Steps and time in daylight — lets your pet walk and sunbathe with you, and fills the Daytime rings.

What Dreamling writes to Apple Health

Nothing is saved. During "Tuck in", Dreamling runs a temporary Mind & Body session only to read live heart rate; it is discarded when the session ends and never stored in Health.

Where your data lives

  • Your journey, pet, and check-ins are stored in a small file on your Apple Watch.
  • Health data is never uploaded, backed up to our servers, or sent to third parties. Dreamling has no servers and no analytics service.
  • Deleting the app deletes this data. You can change Health access at any time in the Health app or Watch Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Purchases

Dreamling Plus is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details.

Notifications

If you allow them, Dreamling sends local reminders (bedtime and good-morning). They are scheduled on your watch; nothing is sent from a server.

Children

Dreamling is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Not medical advice

Dreamling is a wellness and fun companion, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat any condition.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
